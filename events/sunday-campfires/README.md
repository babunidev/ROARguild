# Sunday road bonfires and campfires

**Status: Developing concept — not a confirmed event series.**

## The idea

A possible Sunday gathering would bring members together around bonfires or campfires along roads and paths through Alliance regions.

The intended feeling is simple: travelling companions pause for a while, share stories, enjoy music, meet other players, and spend time together without needing a dungeon, raid, or objective to justify the gathering.

## Why explore it

A recurring social ritual could give the community a familiar point of connection. The gathering should remain open and relaxed rather than becoming an attendance requirement.

## Still open

- Whether the event series will be adopted.
- Its final name and in-game presentation.
- Whether it happens every Sunday or on another cadence.
- Routes, regions, and meeting locations.
- Organizers and any practical responsibilities.
- How roleplay, music, and open participation should work.

No date, route, or schedule is currently confirmed. Update this document only as decisions are made and events are actually organized.
