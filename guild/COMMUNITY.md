# Community principles

**Status: Proposed working baseline — not a formally adopted code of conduct.**

This document is a starting point for discussing how the community should feel and how members should treat one another.

## Intended atmosphere

- Welcome returning players and people who are new to the game or community.
- Treat people with respect, including when playstyles or opinions differ.
- Keep participation voluntary; avoid attendance pressure and unnecessary performance expectations.
- Make room for roleplay, conversation, exploration, and other shared activities.
- Handle disagreements directly and constructively rather than turning them into public spectacle.

## Boundaries still to define

The guild has not yet adopted formal moderation procedures, officer authority, disciplinary steps, or a detailed code of conduct. Those should be discussed and approved before being presented as official policy.

Do not use this draft as a basis for claiming that a particular moderation process is already in force.
