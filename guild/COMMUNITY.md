# Community principles

**Status: Developing working baseline — not a formally adopted code of conduct.**

This document records the intended atmosphere and boundaries for the community. It is a basis for discussion, not a complete moderation policy.

## Intended atmosphere

- Welcome returning players and people who are new to the game or community.
- Treat people with respect, including when playstyles or opinions differ.
- Keep participation voluntary; avoid attendance pressure and unnecessary performance expectations.
- Make room for roleplay, conversation, exploration, and other shared activities.
- Value companionship and shared experiences over prestige or competition.
- Let members contribute in different ways without requiring everyone to play the same role.

## Intended boundaries

The community is not meant to tolerate racism, sexism, harassment, or bullying.

Do not shame members for their character's race, specialization, talents, or playstyle. The point is to share Azeroth, not to police how another person enjoys the game.

Disagreements should be handled constructively rather than turned into public spectacle. The preference is to recognize positive contributions and use rewards or encouragement where possible rather than defaulting to punishment.

## Eldgrim and community standards

The developing Eldgrim concept is expected to operate within these values. It should not punish or shame a player for character choices or playstyle. Its intended authority, moderation boundaries, and decision process are described in [Eldgrim design document](../community-tools/ORACLE.md).

## Boundaries still to define

The guild has not yet adopted formal moderation procedures, officer authority, disciplinary steps, or a complete code of conduct. Those should be discussed and approved before being presented as official policy.

Do not use this draft as a basis for claiming that a particular moderation process is already in force.
