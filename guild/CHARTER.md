# Guild charter

**Status: Developing draft — not formally adopted.**

This document describes the kind of community the project is trying to build. It is not yet a binding rulebook.

## Purpose

Build a welcoming place for people to share their time in World of Warcraft Forever. The first focus is Alliance roleplay, with room for different playstyles, friendships, and shared adventures.

## Guiding spirit

The community should value companionship and creativity over prestige or competition. Belonging should not depend on performance, attendance, or following one narrow way to play.

## Guiding principles

### Welcome people in

Returning players and newcomers should be able to find a place in the community without needing to prove their value first.

### Keep participation voluntary

The guild should not turn attendance, progression, or performance into a condition of belonging. Activities should give people reasons to gather, not obligations to fulfill.

### Make room for different ways to play

Roleplay, exploration, social time, dungeons, raids, and PvP may all contribute to guild life. The balance should grow from the interests of the people involved.

### Respect different characters and playstyles

Do not shame people for their character's race, specialization, talents, or preferred way of playing.

### Keep the community safe to inhabit

Racism, sexism, harassment, and bullying are incompatible with the intended community. Threats, doxxing, scams and repeated disregard for boundaries are also incompatible with guild life. Consequential discipline requires reliable evidence, a proportionate response and a confidential appeal route, including after removal. See the [working governance](GOVERNANCE.md).

### Build the community together

The guild should be shaped through conversation and shared responsibility. Ranks recognize contributions. Appointed responsibilities carry only the permissions needed for the task. Members may organize activities and propose changes without retaliation.

## Adoption

For this draft to take effect, the founder approves an identified version, sets an effective date and publishes it where members can access it. Announce material changes and apply new conduct obligations prospectively. Document any delegated powers separately and activate them only after the required safeguards have been verified.

Record adoption and later amendments in [the decision log](../docs/DECISIONS.md).
