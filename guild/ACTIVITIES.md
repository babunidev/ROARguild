# Activities and shared rituals

This document tracks the kinds of activities that may shape guild life. Ideas are not schedules, and no activity listed here creates an attendance obligation.

## Initial focus

**Alliance roleplay in World of Warcraft Forever — established direction.**

Roleplay is the initial community focus, but members should not be expected to remain in character at all times or to follow a single style of play.

## Possible shared activities

| Activity | Status | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Roleplay | Initial focus | The first community direction. |
| Social gatherings | Developing | Informal time together, conversation, and spontaneous meetings. |
| Exploration | Proposed | Travelling through Alliance regions and discovering places together. |
| Dungeons | Proposed | Group content when members feel like running it. |
| Raids | Proposed | No progression schedule or attendance requirement has been established. |
| PvP | Proposed | An optional activity, depending on member interest. |
| Traveling-hearth gatherings | Developing | A possible Sunday tradition of lighting fires along roads and meeting fellow travelers; see events/sunday-campfires/. |

## Principles for activities

- Activities should support community rather than become obligations.
- The gathering itself can be the purpose; not every event needs a reward or objective.
- A calendar should only be published when dates, times, and organizers are confirmed.
- Do not imply that every member is expected to participate in every activity.
- Keep spontaneous play possible alongside planned events.
