# Amberhearth guild documents

Amberhearth is the founded Alliance roleplaying fellowship within the wider ROAR community on World of Warcraft Forever. These documents describe its social character and its **working** rules; the existence of a draft does not make it a binding charter.

| Document | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| [Charter](CHARTER.md) | The kind of community the guild is intended to be, pending formal ratification |
| [Community](COMMUNITY.md) | Welcome, participation and the shared atmosphere |
| [Governance](GOVERNANCE.md) | Human responsibilities, unresolved authority and practical decisions |
| [Activities](ACTIVITIES.md) | Shared adventures, storytelling and gatherings |

The in-world Eldgrim character and its draft governance framework are maintained in the [Eldgrim repository](https://github.com/babunidev/eldgrim). They do not silently replace Amberhearth's charter or grant unimplemented technical authority.

For Sunday gatherings, see [the bonfire plan](../events/sunday-campfires/README.md). For accepted decisions and unresolved questions, see [the decision register](../docs/DECISIONS.md).
