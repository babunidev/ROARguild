# Governance and responsibilities

**Status: Open — no formal structure has been adopted.**

This document will describe how the guild is organized once the people involved have agreed on a workable model.

## Current direction

An earlier direction favored founding members sharing responsibility as equals and treating the guild as a shared creation. That remains useful context, but it does not define a complete governance system.

## The Oracle concept

The Oracle has been discussed as an autonomous community Game Master with delegated authority over selected guild systems, potentially operating above ordinary guild ranks. The intended scope includes moderation, mutes, rewards and punishments, ranks, guild-bank allocation, quests, and storylines.

This is a developing design direction, not an implemented governance mechanism. The exact permissions, boundaries, relationship to human leadership, and process for reviewing decisions remain open. See [community-tools/ORACLE.md](../community-tools/ORACLE.md).

## Decisions still needed

- Who can make day-to-day human decisions?
- Which decisions require agreement from the founding group?
- How are responsibilities divided and handed over?
- Are officer or specialist roles needed?
- How are disagreements handled?
- How can members contribute ideas without creating unnecessary bureaucracy?
- How does the proposed Oracle authority relate to human responsibilities?
- If future chapters form, how independent should they be?

## Working rule for this document

Do not invent names, ranks, appointments, permissions, or authority. Record a role only after it has been agreed and assigned. Record significant governance decisions in docs/DECISIONS.md.
